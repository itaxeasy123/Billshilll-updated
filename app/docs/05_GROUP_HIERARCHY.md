# 05. Group Hierarchy

## WHAT
The hierarchical classification tree for the Chart of Accounts, rooted in the 4 Primary Groups.

## WHY
Defines whether an account represents an asset, liability, income, or expense, driving automated Trial Balance, Trading Account, P&L, and Balance Sheet generation.

## 4 PRIMARY GROUPS
1. **ASSETS** (Nature: Debit): Current Assets, Fixed Assets, Bank Accounts, Cash-in-hand, Sundry Debtors.
2. **LIABILITIES** (Nature: Credit): Capital Account, Current Liabilities, Sundry Creditors, Duties & Taxes, Provisions.
3. **INCOME** (Nature: Credit): Direct Incomes (Sales), Indirect Incomes (Interest, Rent).
4. **EXPENSES** (Nature: Debit): Direct Expenses (Purchase, Freight), Indirect Expenses (Salary, Office Rent, Depreciation).

## WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE
- The four root primary group identities and their base financial statement classification.
